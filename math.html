⚙️ Settings

📺 Break videos for each world

Each break plays a random recent Short from the world she was just playing. These start with each official channel. You can replace them with your own picks, one per line: a Short or video link, a playlist link, or a channel ID (UC…). Leave a box empty to go back to the default.

Each video plays to the end, and then the Back to math button lights up. Videos only play once the page is hosted online (GitHub Pages), not from a file opened on the computer.

💾 Backup

Progress is saved in this browser only. iPad and laptop keep separate progress, so pick one as the "home" device or move progress with a backup file.

Sound effects are synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files and nothing to license. Fonts: Fredoka (SIL Open Font License) via Google Fonts. Emoji are drawn by her device.
This is a fan-made learning game for home use. It isn't affiliated with Salish Matter, The Apothecary Diaries, Mark Rober, or Crypton Future Media (Hatsune Miku), and it uses no official images.
